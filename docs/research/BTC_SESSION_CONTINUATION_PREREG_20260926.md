# BTC session / pump-pullback continuation — preregistered exploratory protocol

Version: BTC_SESSION_CONTINUATION_RESEARCH_V1_20260926. Research only; not a runtime trading rule, not a whitelist or UI tier. No orders, no changes to bot entry, size, gates, SL or TP. Existing JSON is untouched; independent files under data/research/btc-session-20260926.

Frozen before outcome calculation: 55 full Vietnam days, 2026-08-02 through 2026-09-25. Earlier days are warm-up; data through UTC September 25 supplies final six-hour exits. First 41 days development descriptive sample, final 14 days (September 12–25) held aside chronologically. The hypothesis was suggested after seeing recent markets, so even this chronological holdout is not a truly prospective test. No parameter optimization on either subset.

Universe: top 60 USDT-margined crypto alt contracts by mean July 2026 daily quote volume, minimum 20 July days, BTC benchmark separately. Public Binance Vision UM monthly/daily 5m ZIP archives with SHA256 verification. Universe fixed before August; no selecting today's winners; new listings excluded. Missing/delisted data reported, never replaced by current top coins. Before outcomes were calculated, discovery of TradFi contracts led to explicit exclusion of EQUITY/COMMODITY/INDEX/FOREX/TradFi contracts using a saved exchangeInfo classification snapshot, plus gold-backed PAXG/XAUT. Today's listing status is not a filter; historically ranked contracts missing in today's metadata remain eligible and are flagged. This metadata correction avoids mixing stock session seasonality into crypto. Not a backtest of existing bot orders or actual account PnL.

Sessions in Vietnam time: morning 06:00–07:59, night 20:00–01:59, other hours. Compare rates per hour, not raw event totals. Report per-hour absolute BTC returns, alt mean absolute returns and proportions with +/-2% hourly moves, weekday/weekend and chronological holdout. Use day-cluster resampling for confidence intervals, not independent-coin assumptions. Night grouped by calendar day for this descriptive comparison.

Closed 15m impulse: green LONG/red SHORT; absolute body >=1%; high-low range >=1.8 times mean true range of preceding 14 closed 15m bars; quote volume >=1.8 times preceding 20-bar mean; close within final quarter of range in direction; close breaks preceding 12-bar high/low. All measures exclude future bars. Multiple observations of one event do not count as separate trades.

Then track up to 12 hours: old breakout level B becomes reference support/resistance, ATR fixed at impulse; invalid if 5m close crosses B adversely by >1 ATR. Pullback/rebound must touch B +/-0.5 ATR and close no worse than B -/+0.25 ATR; mean quote volume of latest three 5m bars <=0.8 of impulse per-5m average. At or after this touch, a closed 5m must reverse through preceding three-bar high/low, green/red, volume >=1.2 preceding 20-bar mean, taker direction share >=50%, and remain within 1 ATR of B. SL uses observed retrace extreme plus 0.25 ATR buffer; TP is observed post-impulse extreme available before entry; stop distance 0.3–5%, gross RR >=1.5. The SHORT mirror tests dump→rebound→continuation, not predicting an absolute top after a pump.

Baseline signals generated independently of BTC. Fixed variants filter these same signals: BTC trend (last closed 5m close/EMA13/EMA25 aligned and last 1h return same sign), then BTC impulse (break preceding six 5m highs/lows with volume >=1.2 prior20, any of latest three closed bars). No later entry added when a rejected signal becomes profitable. Report relative-strength variant separately as exploratory if implemented. All features known before entry.

Enter at next 5m open with adverse 0.03% slippage each side and fee 0.05% per side. Notional returns, no leverage multiplier, before funding. Stress deducts an additional 0.14 percentage point roundtrip (approximately 0.30% total costs). No claim these equal actual Binance account fees. Stop and target both touched in one bar => stop first; adverse open gaps use worse open. Max hold 6h, one open trade per symbol/direction-independent, one trade per episode; skip incomplete future/data gaps. Uniform six-hour signal embargo before holdout boundary, irrespective of realized exit. Stops/targets are research execution assumptions, not changes to bot protection.

Report N, win rate, mean/median return, profit factor, stop/target/time counts, 95% day-cluster CI of mean return, and signal-day concentration. Do not call a method profitable with a sparse holdout or CI crossing zero. Session effects are descriptive associations, not proof BTC causes an alt move. Exact future tops/bottoms are unknowable. No new runtime label/card/WHITELIST is introduced.
